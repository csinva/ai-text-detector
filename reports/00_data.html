00. Data for the AI-generated text detector: sources, licences, mixtures and evaluation sets

Summary. 47 datasets and models were audited for commercial use and kept, 34 were dropped (Table 1). The licensed human text pool has 252,073 passages from 16 Common Pile and related sources (2017 or earlier where dated; Table 3). 507,242 AI texts were generated from them with eight Apache-2.0 or MIT models in four modes (Table 4). The training mixtures hold 407k documents and 171M tokens (round 2 final) up to 799k documents and 314M tokens (round 4, Table 6). 9 evaluation sets or families are listed (Table 2), two of them evaluation only because their human text has no stated provenance or they come from commercial tools. Generated 2026-11-18 22:11 UTC.

1. Data and licences

Rule. Text is kept only if its rights trace to an explicit licence that allows commercial use (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, MIT, Apache-2.0, Open Parliament Licence) or to a per-row licence of that kind. It is dropped when the licence is non-commercial, research-only, “other” or undeclared, and when it is scraped third-party text (news, reviews, forum posts, poems, recipes), even if the aggregate dataset card says MIT or Apache, because a card cannot grant rights to text its author does not hold. Machine-written text is dropped when the generator's weights are released under non-commercial or research-only terms (LLaMA-1, OPT, GLM-130B, MPT-30B-Chat). CC BY-SA text carries attribution and share-alike duties. OpenAI and Llama 2 terms forbid using their outputs to train competing generative models, not classifiers. This is an engineering audit from published terms, not legal advice.

2. Human text pool

All human negatives are passages of 500 to 2,200 characters cut at paragraph boundaries from documents that carry an open licence, with text from 2017 or earlier where the source is dated (dated sources: Stack Exchange creation date, peS2o year, arXiv id, Global Voices url). Round 1 started with books, abstracts, PubMed, parliamentary and recipe text; round 2 added Stack Exchange; round 3 Global Voices and US GPO; round 4 case law, more Stack Exchange and PubMed, regulations, peS2o and arXiv full text. Passages are trimmed to sentence ends so that a cut is not a label, and exact duplicates are dropped.

3. Generated AI text

Every passage is assigned by hash to one generator and one task. New text: continue the passage from a human prefix (exact span labels), rewrite it, write a new text for the same register, or write it casually. Humanizer-style: a second model rewrites an AI text to sound human (pure AI, or human prefix plus humanized continuation). Sentence edits: about 30% of the sentences of a human passage are rewritten by a model. Block edits (round 4): 1 to 4 sentence blocks are polished, paraphrased, restyled, compressed or expanded at 10 to 100% coverage. Typography is not a label: curly quotes and dashes are straightened in 55% of AI texts, cut-off generations are trimmed, and texts that match any evaluation text in their first 400 characters are removed. Generators: Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, Phi-4-mini-instruct, OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct, SmolLM3-3B, GPT-OSS-20B, Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507.
