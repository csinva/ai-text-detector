About this detector

The model is voyage-4-nano (Apache-2.0) fine-tuned to give each token a probability of being AI-written and the whole text a score. It was trained only on data with licences that allow commercial use. The page runs it with ONNX Runtime Web (int8 weights, WebAssembly), so no server sees your text.

  • Score. The large number is the model's estimate that the text is AI-written. For text that mixes both, it tracks the share of AI text rather than giving a yes or no.
  • Heatmap. Each word is shaded by the mean probability of its tokens. Short passages and informal writing are the least reliable; on informal human text the false positive rate is substantial.
  • Not for decisions about people. Detectors make mistakes in both directions. Do not use a score as evidence that someone cheated.
  • Limits. Texts over 30,000 characters are truncated. Output of tools that paraphrase AI text to evade detectors is only partly detected.
