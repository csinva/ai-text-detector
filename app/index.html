About this detector

The model is voyage-4-nano (Apache-2.0) fine-tuned to give each token a probability of being AI-written and the whole text a score. It was trained only on data with licences that allow commercial use. The page runs it with ONNX Runtime Web (int8 weights, WebAssembly), so no server sees your text.

  • Live reading. The page re-reads the text a moment after you pause or finish a few words, and right away when you paste. Words still waiting for the model shimmer; a light sweeps across them as they are scored.
  • Score. The large number is the model's estimate that the text is AI-written. For text that mixes both, it tracks the share of AI text rather than giving a yes or no.
  • Not for decisions about people. Short passages, informal writing and text rewritten by paraphrasing tools are the least reliable, and detectors err in both directions. Do not use a score as evidence that someone cheated.
  • Limits. Texts over 30,000 characters are cut.
